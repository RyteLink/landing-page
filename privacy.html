🛡️ Privacy Highlights at a Glance

We never sell your data: RyteLink does not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to advertising networks. All identity verification (BVN/NIN) is collected exclusively to satisfy statutory Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) Anti-Money Laundering (AML) mandates and create your dedicated Providus Bank virtual accounts.

1. Overview & Regulatory Framework

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile applications, web platforms, and associated services provided by RyteLink Technologies, a brand and product of Timgift01 Technologies (registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission of Nigeria, CAC).

We operate in full compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019, the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) Consumer Protection Framework, and international app store developer guidelines including Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1 and Google Play User Data Policies.

2. Information We Collect

To provide financial trade cycle financing, virtual banking accounts, and consumer electronics sourcing, we collect the following categories of information:

Category Examples Statutory Necessity
Identity Data Full legal name, date of birth, residential address, email, phone number, Bank Verification Number (BVN) or National Identity Number (NIN). Mandated by CBN KYC (Know Your Customer) & AML/CFT regulations for financial transactions.
Financial Data Virtual account numbers, linked commercial settlement bank accounts, trade cycle allocations, upfront interest payouts, withdrawal ledger. Required to execute deposits, withdrawals, yield distribution, and ledger accounting.
Device & Telemetry IP address, device model, operating system version, browser user-agent, session identifiers. Fraud prevention, unauthorized session detection, and account protection.
KYC Proof Documents Government ID photo, utility bill proof of address, facial verification snapshot. Tier-2 and Tier-3 wallet compliance and transaction limit elevations.

3. Biometrics & Device Permissions

When utilizing the RyteLink mobile application, we may request access to specific hardware sensors and capabilities:

  • Biometric Authentication (Face ID / Fingerprint): We use Apple LocalAuthentication and Android BiometricPrompt APIs solely to unlock your app and authorize fund transfers. Your raw biometric patterns never leave your personal device; they are processed inside your device's Secure Enclave or hardware Keystore.
  • Camera Access (NSCameraUsageDescription): Used only when you choose to photograph identity documents during KYC verification or capture your profile photo.
  • Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription): Used only when you select an existing document or proof of address to upload.
  • Push Notifications: Used to deliver instant real-time transactional alerts, deposit receipts, and trade cycle maturity updates.

4. Purpose & Legal Basis of Processing

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, every data processing activity must rest upon a lawful basis:

  • Performance of Contract: To open your wallet, assign dedicated Providus Bank virtual accounts, manage physical inventory cycles, and execute withdrawals.
  • Legal & Regulatory Obligation: To satisfy CBN, NFIU, and NDPR compliance mandates, including transaction monitoring, tax reporting, and anti-fraud investigations.
  • Legitimate Interests: To optimize system reliability, detect credential stuffing, enforce rate limits, and safeguard customer balances.

5. Banking & Custody Partners

RyteLink Technologies is a financial technology company and not a deposit-taking commercial bank. Virtual accounts and settlement rails are powered by:

  • Anchor BaaS (Anchor Technologies): CBN-approved Banking-as-a-Service infrastructure provider facilitating automated virtual account creation and NIP outward transfers.
  • Providus Bank PLC: A commercial bank licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria. Customer virtual accounts held at Providus Bank are insured up to the statutory limit by the Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC).

We share only the minimum required information (name, BVN verification token) with our regulated banking partners to establish and maintain your virtual accounts.

6. Data Retention & Statutory Audit Logs

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was gathered.

⚠️ Statutory Financial Retention Rules

In accordance with the Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022 and Central Bank of Nigeria regulations, financial institutions and fintech platforms are statutorily required to maintain records of customer identification and all financial transaction journals for a minimum of five (5) to seven (7) years following the termination of an account. Non-financial marketing preferences and session tokens are purged immediately upon account closure.

7. In-App Account Deletion Flow (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v))

In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v) and Google Play policy, RyteLink provides a straightforward, fully automated in-app mechanism to permanently delete and close your account without requiring third-party emails or support tickets:

  1. Log in to the RyteLink Mobile App.
  2. Navigate to Profile → Account Settings (or Legal & Privacy).
  3. Select "Close & Delete Account" under the Danger Zone section.
  4. Review the closure disclosures and ensure your liquid balance is below ₦100 (funds must be withdrawn prior to closure to protect your assets).
  5. Authorize the action with your secret 4-digit transaction PIN or account password.

Upon authorization, your active login tokens and Redis sessions are revoked immediately, your profile status is marked as closed, Supabase authentication is deactivated, and an automated security confirmation email is sent to your registered address.

8. Data Security & Encryption Standards

We enforce enterprise-grade security controls to protect your personal and financial information:

  • Transport Security: 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption for all data in transit across our APIs and web interfaces.
  • PIN & Password Cryptography: Transaction PINs are hashed using individual cryptographic salts and PBKDF2/Argon2 algorithms; plain text PINs are never stored.
  • Double-Entry Ledger Integrity: All wallet balance changes are recorded across strict double-entry ledger journals to prevent reconciliation tampering.
  • Strict Rate Limiting & Anomaly Detection: Automated Redis-backed shields prevent brute-force attacks against authentication and withdrawal endpoints.

9. Your Privacy Rights Under NDPR & NDPA

As a data subject in Nigeria, you are entitled to comprehensive legal rights:

  • Right of Access: You can request an electronic export of all personal data held about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can update inaccurate personal or residential information directly in the app.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: You can opt out of promotional communications at any time.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request your transaction history in structured CSV/PDF formats.
  • Right to Lodge Complaints: If you believe your data has been mishandled, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at ndpc.gov.ng.
