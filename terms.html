2. Account Registration, Security & Transaction PIN

When creating a RyteLink account, you must provide truthful, complete, and current information. You agree to maintain the security and confidentiality of your credentials:

  • Transaction PIN Secrecy: You will be prompted to establish a 4-digit secret transaction PIN. This PIN authorizes all fund withdrawals and account changes. RyteLink staff will never ask for your PIN or password.
  • Device Authentication: You are responsible for ensuring that unauthorized persons do not access your unlocked mobile device.
  • Notification of Breach: If you suspect unauthorized access to your account or credentials, you must notify RyteLink support immediately at info@timgift.com.

3. Virtual Banking Services & Settlement Rails

RyteLink provides dedicated virtual account numbers to enable seamless funding of your wallet:

  • Banking Partner: Banking services and virtual account infrastructure are powered by Anchor BaaS in partnership with Providus Bank PLC (a commercial bank licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria).
  • NDIC Insurance: Eligible deposits held within commercial partner accounts are protected under the statutory deposit insurance scheme administered by the Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC).
  • Inward & Outward Transfers: Inward bank transfers credit your Liquid Wallet in real time via NIBSS Instant Payments (NIP). Outward transfers to external Nigerian bank accounts are subject to CBN transaction limits and anti-fraud verification checks.

4. Trade Cycle Financing Agreement

RyteLink operates asset-backed inventory trade cycles that finance the bulk procurement of verified physical consumer electronics (e.g., Apple MacBooks, iPhones, Dell XPS workstations) directly from authorized North American distributors:

  • Cycle Duration: Standard trade cycles have a fixed maturity period of 3 months (90 days) from the allocation date.
  • Minimum Allocation: Users may participate in trade cycles starting from a minimum of ₦5,000.
  • Physical Asset Backing: All funds committed to a trade cycle are allocated to physical electronics inventory held in insured bonded warehouses or transit custody.
  • Principal Return: At the conclusion of the 90-day cycle, 100% of your principal capital is automatically unlocked and credited to your Liquid Wallet, ready for immediate withdrawal or reinvestment.

5. Upfront Interest Payouts

Unlike conventional savings schemes that defer interest payouts to maturity, RyteLink offers an instant upfront yield:

⚡ Upfront Yield Policy

Upon confirming funding of a 3-month trade cycle, your agreed upfront yield (10% of invested capital) is credited immediately on Day 1 directly into your Liquid Wallet. You are entitled to withdraw this upfront interest immediately to any Nigerian bank account.

Because upfront yield is distributed on Day 1, early premature liquidation of a trade cycle prior to the 90-day maturity date is subject to early-exit penalties or requires holding until cycle completion.

6. Electronics Hardware Store & Procurement

Through our commercial integration with Timgift.com, users can purchase genuine North American specification consumer electronics directly within the app or online:

  • Product Authenticity: All devices are authentic US factory-unlocked hardware sourced directly from verified North American distributors.
  • Shipping & Logistics: Orders include insured international air-freight and nationwide last-mile delivery to Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, and other states.
  • Inspection Window: Buyers are granted a 48-hour inspection window upon parcel delivery to verify item specifications and physical condition before purchase confirmation closes.
