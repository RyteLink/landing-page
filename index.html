Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about upfront interest, trade cycle financing, and product sourcing.

Unlike traditional banks or mutual funds that make you wait until the end of an investment cycle, RyteLink calculates and credits your interest immediately upon successful funding of a verified trade cycle. You can withdraw this interest directly into any Nigerian commercial bank account on Day 1.

To keep the platform accessible while maintaining institutional order volume, the minimum amount to participate in a trade cycle is ₦5,000, and the maximum is ₦10,000,000 per user per tranche. Tier upgrades (BVN and NIN verification) unlock higher limits.

Your initial capital principal matures in 3 months (90 days) for standard trade cycles (or 6 months for designated curated bulk tranches). Upon maturity date, 100% of your capital is automatically unlocked and credited to your Liquid Wallet, ready for immediate withdrawal or rollover into the next trade cycle.

A trade cycle represents physical bulk purchases of verified, fast-moving consumer electronics (MacBooks, iPhones, gaming hardware, and corporate workstation equipment) sourced directly from North American authorized suppliers. RyteLink finances the procurement, coordinates insured freight, and sells to pre-contracted corporate and retail buyers across Africa.

You can shop directly through our integrated storefront at Timgift.com or within the RyteLink app. We provide genuine North American spec products with express air-cargo delivery to your doorstep in Lagos, Abuja, or anywhere in Nigeria.

Safety is our top priority. User wallet and virtual accounts are powered through licensed Banking-as-a-Service (BaaS) infrastructure provided by Anchor, held with CBN-licensed partner commercial banks. All trade assets are insured against transit and warehouse hazards, and customer accounts are protected with 2FA, biometric authentication, and double-entry ledger audits.
